hong2p.stats module

Statistics of paired values and of grouped matrices: residuals around linear and monotone fits, rank and linear correlations of aligned Series, and resampling of the rows (e.g. recordings, or flies) of grouped matrices (bootstrap, split halves).

Works on any Series sharing an index, e.g. the pair Series of hong2p.rdm, and on DataFrames whose rows are the units resampled (one row per recording, columns e.g. odor pairs).

Moved from the KC-remapping experiment queue (model_experiment_queue’s kcq.harness), 2026-11-12, with the resampling generalized from Yang’s recordings / flies to any rows or clusters of rows.

Returns DataFrame indexed by statistic: ‘observed’ (fn of the groups’ means), ‘boot_mean’, ‘boot_sd’, ‘lo’, ‘hi’ (NaN draws ignored).

hong2p.stats.resample_group_means(groups, rng, *, cluster=None)[source]

Returns one bootstrap replicate of each group’s mean row.

Parameters:

  • groups (Mapping[str, DataFrame]) – {name: DataFrame}, rows the units resampled (e.g. recordings), columns the statistics averaged (e.g. odor pairs).

  • rng (Generator) – draws the resamples, group by group in groups’ order.

  • cluster (Sequence[str] | None) – index levels whose unique combinations are resampled instead of rows, each drawn cluster keeping all its rows (e.g. (‘date’, ‘fly_num’) to draw flies, keeping both hemispheres of each, which are not independent). None draws rows.

Return type:

Dict[str, Series]

Each group’s draws are with replacement, as many as it has rows (or clusters); the drawn rows are then averaged with equal weight.

hong2p.stats.split_half_reliability(rows, *, n_splits=200, seed=0, method='spearman')[source]

Returns the reliability of the mean row of a DataFrame, from random split halves of its rows (e.g. of the mean odor-pair distance matrix over recordings).

Keys: ‘n_recordings’ (rows), ‘split_half’ (the correlation between the two halves’ means, mean over splits; method ‘spearman’ or ‘pearson’), ‘split_half_lo’ / ‘_hi’ (its 5th / 95th percentiles), ‘spearman_brown_full’ (2r / (1 + r), for the full set of rows) and ‘ceiling’ (its square root: the expected correlation with the observed mean of a noise-free model that matched the true mean perfectly).

Return type:

Dict[str, float]
