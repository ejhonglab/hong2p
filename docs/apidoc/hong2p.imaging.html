hong2p.imaging module

Imaging time courses and trial-to-trial noise: per-trial excerpts of traces around odor onsets and the responses in windows after them, the calcium indicator’s kernel (fitted to mean traces under a drive), and odor-odor distances corrected for the noise between trials.

Traces are (units, volumes) arrays; trials of one recording are (n_trials, units, odors) arrays (trial i of every odor is the i-th presentation, or repeat, of that odor). Distances are 1 - cosine similarity across units, returned as pair Series (hong2p.rdm).

Moved from the KC-remapping experiment queue (model_experiment_queue’s kcq.harness), 2026-11-12; the volume rate, the windows and the odors are arguments.

hong2p.imaging.fit_indicator_kernel(t, y, drive='onset', *, pulse=2.0, edges=None, weights=None, rise_bounds=(0.05, 10.0), decay_bounds=(0.5, 200.0), fixed=None)[source]

Returns the least-squares fit of amplitude * kernel_signal(t, rise, decay, drive) to a mean trace y.

The amplitude is solved linearly for each (rise, decay) (and kept >= 0); rise and decay are fitted on a log scale within their bounds from several starts. With fixed = (rise, decay) only the amplitude is fitted (e.g. a kernel fitted on one trace tested on another).

Returns dict: ‘rise’, ‘decay’, ‘amplitude’, ‘rms’ (root mean squared residual, in y’s units), ‘n’, ‘rise_at_bound’, ‘decay_at_bound’ (within 1 % of a bound), ‘drive’.

hong2p.imaging.noise_corrected_pair_distances(trials, odors, min_reliability=None, order=None, *, names=('odor_a', 'odor_b'))[source]

Returns the noise-corrected cosine distances of one recording from its trials.

Each odor’s signal power is the squared length of its trial mean minus the trial-to-trial variance summed over units divided by the number of trials (floored at 1e-12): independent noise adds that much to the squared length of a mean and nothing, in expectation, to the dot product of two odors’ means. The corrected distance is 1 - (dot product of the trial means) / sqrt(signal power a x signal power b).

Parameters:

  • trials (ndarray) – (n_trials, units, odors).

  • odors (Sequence) – the odors of the last axis.

  • min_reliability (float | None) – pairs with an odor whose signal power is less than this share of its trial mean’s squared length are NaN (the correction divides by a near-zero signal there).

  • order (Sequence | None) – canonical order of the odors (hong2p.rdm.order_items).

Return type:

Series

Returns a canonical pair Series.

hong2p.imaging.noise_free_pair_distances(trials, odors, *, repeats='different', values='graded', thresh=None, order=None, names=('odor_a', 'odor_b'))[source]

Returns the noise-free cosine distances of one recording, from products of single trials of different repeats.

For each pair (a, b): 1 - C_ab / sqrt(C_aa C_bb), C_ab the mean single-trial cosine between odor a in trial i and odor b in trial j over the trial pairs i != j selected by repeats, C_aa the same for odor a with itself. Noise independent between trials adds nothing, in expectation, to such a product, whether or not it is shared by the odors of a trial; a drift common to an odor’s trials acts on C_ab and C_aa alike and cancels in the ratio. Not clipped: a near-identical pair can come out slightly below 0. (Cosines rather than dot products, so a gain of a whole presentation, which a cosine does not see, does not weigh a trial in.)

Parameters:

  • trials (ndarray) – (n_trials, units, odors).

  • odors (Sequence) – the odors of the last axis.

  • repeats (str) – ‘different’ (every i != j), ‘adjacent’ (|i - j| == 1) or ‘first_third’ (|i - j| == 2).

  • values (str) – how the single trials are read (trial_values).

  • thresh (float | None) – how the single trials are read (trial_values).

  • order (Sequence | None) – canonical order of the odors (hong2p.rdm.order_items).

Return type:

Series

Returns a canonical pair Series.
